A blog is a stack: the newest thing sits on top and everything else sinks. A garden is a place. Things get planted where they fit, they grow at their own pace, and you can come back and find them where you left them.

Every piece here has a stage. A seedling is a rough thought that might not survive. Something growing has been tended a few times. An evergreen is about as done as anything here gets. On the map, the stage is the size of the plant.

New pieces get planted near the ones they talk to. Over time the map should say something the list can't.
